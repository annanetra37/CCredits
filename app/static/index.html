Reconciliation

Every figure on this portal is produced by SQL views. This recomputes the same figures a second time in Python — from the Bronze cells and from a fresh parse of the stored original files, never from the views — and compares them. Two independent implementations agreeing is the evidence that the transfer to Gold is faithful.
